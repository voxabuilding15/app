# FocusFlow Privacy Policy

_Last updated: [date]_

FocusFlow ("the app") is designed to work entirely on your device.

## What we collect

**Nothing.** The app has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no crash-reporting service, and it makes no network requests. We cannot see your data.

## What the app stores on your device

Tasks, habits, calendar events, finance entries, notes (including attachments and voice recordings), focus sessions, achievements and settings are stored in the app's private storage on your phone. Backups you create are files on your device (or wherever you choose to save or share them).

## Permissions and why they are used

- **Notifications and exact alarms** – to show reminders, alarms and timer alerts at the right time.
- **Microphone** – only when you record a voice note; the recording stays on your device.
- **Biometrics** – to unlock locked notes; Android performs the check and the app never receives your biometric data.
- **Foreground service / audio** – to keep ambient focus sounds playing with the screen off.
- **Vibration, run at start-up** – to vibrate when a timer ends and to restore reminders after a restart.

## Sharing

The app never sends your data anywhere. If you export a backup, a statistics report, a note attachment or feedback, the file or text is handed to the app you choose in Android's share sheet; from then on that app's privacy policy applies.

## Android backup

Android's automatic cloud backup is turned off for this app so your notes and finances are not uploaded without you knowing. Use the in-app backup if you want a copy.

## Deleting your data

Open Settings → Data management to erase data inside the app, or uninstall the app to remove everything it stored.

## Children

The app is not directed to children under 13 and collects no personal information from anyone.
